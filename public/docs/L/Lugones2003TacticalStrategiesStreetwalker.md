## Claim 2: Resistant intentionality is intersubjective

**Author argument / evidence.** Intentions may lie between subjects and overlapping worlds of sense, reaching provisional completion through dispersed collective work rather than a controlling strategist (pp. 208–209, 216–217). **Warrant.** The origin and development of intention cannot always be located fully within one independent person. **Boundary.** Lugones does not dispense with intention or claim that all collective activity resists oppression. **My inference.** Distinguish evidence of group deliberation and evolving decisions from later claims that one individual conceived a project. **Practice cross-check.** TODO: triangulate DDR minutes, letters, reports and retrospective testimony.

**Author argument / evidence.** Lugones calls effective autonomous agency an illusion backed by the privileges and language of institutional power; she proposes active subjectivity to discuss resistance that this model excludes (pp. 210–211). **Warrant.** Individual achievement can appear independent when conditions of recognition and support recede from view. **Boundary.** This cannot establish the degree or direction of credit misattribution to any DDR figure. **My inference.** Separate named leadership from documented actions, collaborators and infrastructures of knowledge-making. **Practice cross-check.** TODO: compare authors, acknowledgements, personnel and project outputs.

**Author argument / evidence.** Lugones revisits de Certeau's tactic/strategy distinction, arguing that bodily proximity and sensory contact can yield complex understanding of social relations (pp. 212–215). **Warrant.** Seeing 'from above' is not the only route to depth; abstraction can itself misrepresent a living setting. **Boundary.** Political street-level resistance is not interchangeable with a designer's informal practice. **My inference.** Investigate whether situated teaching and collaborative making produced concepts overlooked by formal DDR methodological descriptions. **Practice cross-check.** TODO: oral histories and dated teaching documentation.

# Definitions and benchmark

**Tactical strategy:** embodied, socially perceptive practice that contests a binary between strategic mastery and momentary tactics (pp. 207–209, 214–216).

**Active subjectivity:** a category for resistant meaningful activity that institutionally backed models of autonomous agency fail to recognize (pp. 210–211, 215–219).

**Hangouts:** permeable spaces in which collective meaning and resistant intention can form (pp. 209, 220–221).

**My benchmark:** Use the chapter where evidence allows a meaningful distinction among documented action, credited authorship, attributed intention and inferred contribution. Never turn missing attribution into positive proof of historical participation.

# Thesis integration and synthesis

Chapter 3 requires that plurality be built into theory; Chapter 4 develops relational attention to others' situated worlds; Chapter 7 exposes recognitions that project familiar categories upon unfamiliar people. Chapter 10 completes this argument by asking who is recognized as an intending, knowledgeable actor and what institutionally unsupported collective practices are overlooked. In the DDR thesis, it opens a critical question about whether archival organization and historiography privilege articulated programmes, formal authorship and named leadership over informal and relational knowledge production. That proposition remains empirically open: documents, roles and oral histories must establish particular contributions, and no computational retrieval should invent the absent links.

**Reusable thesis sentence:** The absence of individually attributed intention does not itself imply an absence of meaningful collective contribution; nor does it provide sufficient evidence to assign such a contribution retrospectively.

**Boundary and risk:** Lugones theorizes oppression and resistance, not systematic design methodology. An overextended analogy would erase her political specificity while treating every trace of informal work as resistant.
